Our Royal Heritage

Founded under the visionary stewardship of Parshwa Enterprise, our flagship brand Princess Jewellery has stood as a beacon of purity, imperial aesthetics, and master craftsmanship for more than 20 years.

Rooted in the prestigious bullion and jewelry corridors of Ahmedabad (Gujarat) and Zaveri Bazaar (Mumbai), our atelier crafts timeless treasures for discerning brides, generational families, and prestigious wholesale export partners worldwide. Every ornament is born from the skilled hands of master karigars (traditional artisans) whose lineage of fine goldsmithing spans generations.

Our Vision

To be the premier royal jewelry atelier of India and a globally respected export powerhouse, renowned for preserving ancient Indian goldsmithing art while redefining modern bridal luxury with 100% verified metallurgical purity.

Our Goal

To empower every bride, jewelry lover, and bulk buyer with absolute peace of mind through BIS 916 Hallmark certification, honest transparent pricing, competitive live Ahmedabad market rates, and heirloom pieces that appreciate in value across generations.

Our Expertise

Specialized in manufacturing 24K pure bullion, 22K (916) Hallmark temple ornaments, bridal chokers, uncut Polki, delicate Suniya Bali earrings, and Imperial Gents rings, backed by 20+ years of precision export compliance.

Our Areas of Specialized Expertise

100% BIS Hallmark Purity

Every piece is certified with government-recognized 6-digit HUID laser hallmarking for guaranteed karat purity.

Master Karigar Craftsmanship

Over 120 hours of hand-filigree, stone-setting, and micro-embossing on bespoke bridal creations.

Global Wholesale Exports

Trusted export partner supplying fine Indian jewelry to clients across 50+ countries including UAE, USA, and UK.

Live Ahmedabad Bullion Transparency

Real-time pricing aligned directly with the Ahmedabad Bullion Market, ensuring honest value with 0% exchange deductions.
