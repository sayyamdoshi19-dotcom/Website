## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the regal heritage of Indian royal gold craftsmanship filtered through the disciplined restraint of an international luxury fashion atelier. The emotional response is one of reverence, timeless refinement, and tactile exclusivity—evoking the quiet intimacy of a private salon suite in an opulent palace.

The style unites **Minimalist Haute Luxury** with **Subtle Regal Ornamentation**. The UI prioritizes generous spatial staging, hairline filigree borders, whisper-soft champagne tones, and museum-grade product presentation. Visual richness is derived not from heavy digital decoration, but from hyper-deliberate kerning, warm metallic accents, pristine contrast against ivory surfaces, and restrained royal motifs rendered with surgical clarity.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from royal heirloom gold and heritage bridal velvet, anchored against architectural stone surfaces:

- **Primary / Royal Gold (`#C6A15B`):** Used for focal metallic accents, active interactive highlights, curated badges, refined dividers, and bespoke decorative glyphs.
- **Secondary / Deep Burgundy (`#4A101C`):** Evokes imperial velvet and heritage rubies. Applied to high-prestige CTAs, curated editorial tags, critical touchpoints, and ceremonial announcement bands.
- **Tertiary / Champagne Beige (`#E8DDC8`):** A soft warm metallic wash for secondary containers, subtle banner backgrounds, table alternating lines, and tonal layering.
- **Neutral / Charcoal Black (`#191716`):** Deep obsidian charcoal replaces harsh synthetic black for editorial typography, high-contrast states, and commanding headers.
- **Base Surfaces:** Canvas sits on **Ivory White (`#FAF7F0`)** to impart organic warmth, while cards and interactive drawers transition to **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)** to spotlight gold jewellery photography with natural brilliance.

Avoid harsh saturation. Gradients should be used sparingly, limited to micro-shimmer linear highlights (`linear-gradient(135deg, #DFC07A 0%, #C6A15B 50%, #9F7B33 100%)`) on selected metallic callouts.

## Typography

The typography pairs the architectural, high-contrast grace of Playfair Display with the clean, utilitarian clarity of Inter. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Headings, collection titles, and editorial narratives utilize Playfair Display. Display styles and titles should consistently be set in uppercase or structured title case with generous letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to capture the authoritative pacing of high-fashion publications.
- **Body & Captions:** Inter provides unobtrusive readability at light and normal weights (`300` and `400`), preventing serif density from exhausting the reader during jewellery specification perusal.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Small tags, karats, purity marks (e.g., 22KT / 916), prices, and navigation labels employ uppercase Inter with extended tracking (`0.14em` to `0.22em`) to mirror luxury hallmark stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional 12-column grid system anchored by expansive outer margins that simulate the wide mats of framed archival art pieces.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a minimum outer margin of 64px (`4rem`), scaling up to a locked max-width container of 1440px. Asymmetrical editorial compositions are encouraged: featured solitary ornaments span 7 columns, paired with 5 columns of curated poetic narrative.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) margins. Staggered card layouts collapse to balanced 2-column or 4-column groupings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Product displays shift into sweeping horizontal edge-to-edge carousels with peak offsets.

Spacing between major sections relies on generous breathing room (`space-xl` and above) to evoke quiet opulence. Dense clusters of controls are prohibited; white space is treated as an active luxury material.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is articulated through **Tonal Layering** and **Soft Amber-Tinted Ambient Shadows**, rejecting harsh modern drop shadows in favor of ambient light reflecting off warm gold.

- **Flat/Resting State:** The canvas sits at `#FAF7F0` (Ivory White). Elevated elements such as showcase cards and bespoke modal sheets lift onto `#FFFFFF` (Pure White), framed by a hairline perimeter border (`1px solid rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.25)`).
- **Floating Surfaces & Hover:** Hover states and dropdown drawers rely on an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with soft umber and gold: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(74, 16, 28, 0.05), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.1)`.
- **Modals & Overlays:** Background scrims utilize Deep Burgundy at low opacity (`rgba(25, 23, 22, 0.6)` combined with a subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`), turning background compositions into a soft, dreamlike focus.
- **Metallic Dividers:** Section separators use razor-thin golden gradients that feather transparently into the background: `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.4), transparent)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Sharp (Level 0)** shape geometry (`0px` border-radius). 

Sharp, architectural edges reflect the precision cut of precious stones, traditional gold ingot corners, and bespoke luxury packaging. Buttons, input fields, image frames, and promotional banners maintain crisp 90-degree right angles. 

To introduce artisanal organic softness without rounding corners:
- Micro-elements (such as bridal floral glyphs or filigree separators) provide ornamental curvature inside sharp structural frames.
- Select floating round badges (e.g., gold hallmark stamps or karat purity seals) are permitted complete geometric circularity (`rounded-full`), establishing contrast with the rigid architectural frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury (Burgundy Velvet):** Crisp rectangular container with `#4A101C` background, `#FAF7F0` uppercase text, `0.14em` tracking, and a subtle border `1px solid #4A101C`. On hover: transition to `#191716` with a delicate outer gold aura.
- **Secondary (Royal Gold Outline):** Transparent background, `1px solid #C6A15B`, text in `#C6A15B` or `#191716`. On hover: fills completely with `#C6A15B` and transitions text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Editorial Text Button:** Borderless; Playfair or tracked Inter with a self-drawing bottom border in gold that expands outwards from center on hover.

### Cards & Jewellery Showcases
- **Showcase Cards:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a razor `1px solid rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.2)`. Generous inner padding (`space-lg`). Imagery maintains museum-grade light ivory backgrounds.
- **Hover Micro-interaction:** Subtle zoom (`scale: 1.02`) on jewellery photography, with card border intensifying to `rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.6)` alongside a diffuse warm shadow.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Minimalist line or enclosed box style. Baseline underline or border in `rgba(25, 23, 22, 0.2)` against ivory white. Floating label in Inter label-md. Upon focus, border shifts to `#C6A15B` with zero ring glow.
- **Dropdowns:** Custom sharp menus clad in pure white, decorated with a micro gold diamond arrow indicator.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 16px sharp squares, `1px solid #C6A15B`. Active state displays a crisp `#C6A15B` fill with an ivory tick.
- **Radio Buttons:** Sharp concentric diamonds or custom circular gem-markers with a solid `#4A101C` inner dot when selected.

### Chips & Badges
- **Hallmark Tags:** Low-contrast champagne background (`#E8DDC8`), dark charcoal text, tracked uppercase `10px`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.4)`. Used exclusively for purity grades (e.g., "22K BIS HALLMARKED", "KUNDAN JADAU").

### Specialized Jewellery Components
- **Bespoke Motifs & Dividers:** Subtle, royal Indian jali or lotus line motifs rendered as inline SVGs in `#C6A15B` used to demarcate bridal suites and heritage collections.
- **Artisan Ingot Swatches:** Karat selector chips styled as miniature brushed metal plates (22KT, 18KT, 14KT) with tactile border-states.
