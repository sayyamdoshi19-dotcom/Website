Ancestral Metallurgy

The Anatomy of Royal Craft

Four distinct disciplines harmonized to create jewellery that endures as a sovereign family heirloom.

01

Hand-Carved Wax Moulds

Every royal motif begins with lost-wax sculpture (Cire Perdue), hand-chiselled under magnifying lenses by veteran sculptors.

architecture Micro Sculpting
02

Precision Die-Stamping

22-karat solid gold ingots undergo cold-hammering and hand-die stamping to achieve structural density without unnecessary weight.

hardware 22KT Structural Integrity
03

Delicate Jaali Filigree

Threads of gold as thin as silk are hand-twisted and micro-soldered into intricate lattice patterns reminiscent of Mughal palace screens.

blur_on 0.2mm Wire Lace
04

Agate Stone Hand-Buffing

Finished jewels are hand-burnished using natural agate burnishing stones, bringing out the hypnotic, deep golden mirror sheen.

arrow_back_ios_new Natural Mirror Luster
