Origin & Provenance

PRINCESS JEWELS celebrates the timeless beauty of gold through designs inspired by Indian heritage and contemporary elegance.

Our ateliers are defined by an unhurried reverence for gold. What began as a private commission atelier crafting celebratory adornments for the royal court has gently evolved into a global sanctum for connoisseurs of pure high jewellery.

In our philosophy, 22-karat gold is not merely an alloy of unmatched warmth; it is a sacred conduit of emotion, honouring the sovereign grace of the women who wear it. Each creation is brought to fruition by generational karigars whose hands preserve the cadence of royal dynasties—transforming solid metal into delicate golden lace that moves fluidly with the human body.

By fusing the regal geometry of centuries-old temple carvings with the clean, architectural silhouettes favoured by modern tastemakers, we construct timeless heirlooms that speak fluently across generations.

The Art of Our Craftsmanship

A close-up examination of the four time-honoured techniques that define every royal bespoke commission.

Jaipur & Mysore Master Ateliers
Technique 01 Engraving & Relief

Hand-Chased Embossing (Nakshi)

Using traditional miniature punches and iron hammers, our master artisans gently coax sculptured relief directly from sheets of pure gold. No machine stamps can recreate the living, breathing organic depth of hand-executed Nakshi carvings.

Technique 02 Openwork Lattice

Delicate Wire Filigree (Jali Craft)

Fine threads of solid gold are hand-twisted and microscopic coils are patiently arranged into lightweight, lace-like screens. This ancient technique mirrors the perforated stone jali panels of Indian royal pavilions.

Technique 03 Gem Insertion

Traditional Jadau & Kundan Setting

Uncut Polki gems and cabochon emeralds are enveloped in refined 24K gold foil without claws or prongs. The reverse surfaces are adorned with delicate Meenakari enamelling, creating secret personal art visible solely to the wearer.

Technique 04 Finishing & Polish

Diamond-Burnishing & Mirror Polish

Every curve undergoes final burnishing with natural agate stones and ultra-fine diamond pastes, unlocking the warm, glowing depth unique to high-purity yellow gold without compromising structural crispness.
